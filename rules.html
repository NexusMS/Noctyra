Community Guidelines

Noctyra Rules

Simple guidelines that keep Noctyra welcoming, safe, and enjoyable for everyone.

BEFORE YOU JUMP IN

Keep Noctyra A Great Place

These rules aren't here to slow anyone down — they just keep Noctyra a fun, respectful place to hang out. Most of it comes down to common sense: be cool to each other, play fair, and use good judgment. A quick read now saves everyone a headache later.

THE RULES

Community Guidelines

Six simple categories that cover everything you need to know.

🤝

Respect Everyone

Treat members, staff, and newcomers with respect. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or personal attacks.

💬

Keep Chat Clean

Avoid spam, excessive flooding, unnecessary mentions, and disruptive behavior. Use the appropriate channels for conversations.

🎮

Fair Play

No cheating, exploiting, abusing bugs, or intentionally ruining other players' experiences.

🛡️

No Scams

Do not scam, deceive, impersonate, or attempt to steal from other members. Be especially careful with trades and giveaways.

🚫

No NSFW or Dangerous Content

Keep the community appropriate and safe. Do not share explicit, excessively violent, or otherwise inappropriate content.

⚖️

Follow Staff & Discord Rules

Follow reasonable instructions from moderators and respect Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

MODERATION

Rules Are Enforced To Protect The Community

Rules are enforced to protect the community, not to punish people unnecessarily. Depending on the severity and frequency of a violation, moderators may issue a warning, mute, kick, or ban. Our staff always try to be fair and reasonable when handling any situation.

Be Part of Noctyra

Play Fair. Stay Respectful. Have Fun.

That's really all it takes. Follow the guidelines, look out for each other, and enjoy your time in Noctyra.
